# Privacy Policy — Rajneeti Decoded Pipeline

Last updated: 2026-10-20

This application is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel
**Rajneeti Decoded**. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, via OAuth 2.0, to upload
the channel's own videos and update their metadata.

- No third-party user data is collected, stored or shared.
- OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's device and are never sent to any third party.
- No data obtained through Google APIs is sold or transferred to third parties, except as needed
  to upload content to YouTube.
- You can revoke access at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
